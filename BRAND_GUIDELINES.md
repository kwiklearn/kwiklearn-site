# KwikLearn Brand Guidelines

Authoritative reference for all KwikLearn content: website copy, articles, videos, social posts, store listings, outreach and AI-generated material. Last updated 2026-11-24.

**Permanent principle: BECE and WASSCE preparation is KwikLearn's initial market entry strategy, not the entirety of its educational mission.**

Before writing any public content, also read `docs/company-contact-policy.md` in the app repository for the email addresses (info@ for general and partnership enquiries, support@ for support, privacy and deletion).

## 1. Identity

| | |
|---|---|
| Company | **KwikLearn Technologies Ltd**, a registered Ghanaian software development company. It builds mobile applications and digital solutions for individuals, businesses and organisations, and its own software products. |
| Platform | **KwikLearn**, a growing digital education platform developed by KwikLearn Technologies Ltd. |
| Slogan | **Learn. Practise. Improve. Achieve.** (unchanged) |
| Website | https://kwiklearn.app |

Always keep the two apart: *KwikLearn Technologies Ltd* is the company, *KwikLearn* is the educational platform. Do not call the platform "a registered company" or the company "an app".

## 2. Mission and vision

- **Mission:** To make quality education more accessible, affordable, engaging and effective through digital technology.
- **Vision:** To build a comprehensive online learning institution that supports students, teachers, parents and educational institutions across Ghana, Africa and eventually beyond.

The vision is a long-term goal. KwikLearn is **not** an accredited school, university or educational institution today and must never be described as one.

## 3. Positioning

KwikLearn is a digital education platform, not a Mathematics app and not only an exam-prep app. Position it around: digital education, structured learning, academic development, examination preparation, educational accessibility, technology-driven learning, school and institutional partnerships, and long-term educational opportunity.

BECE and WASSCE preparation is the *starting point* ("Our journey begins with..."). Lead with the platform and mission; name BECE and WASSCE as where we start. Keep exam terms in titles, descriptions and keywords so existing search visibility is kept, but never as the whole company identity.

Mathematics is accurate where it describes content that is genuinely available. Do not remove it. Do not let it define the brand.

## 4. Audiences

1. Students and learners (the only audience the current app serves directly).
2. Teachers and academic professionals.
3. Parents and families.
4. Schools and educational institutions: private schools, junior and senior high schools, proprietors, headteachers and principals, academic directors, examination preparation centres, educational NGOs.

## 5. Voice and tone

Clear, warm, encouraging, practical and credible. Plain English suited to Ghanaian students, parents and teachers. Confident about the mission, modest about the present. Prefer "we are building", "our journey begins", "we aim to". Avoid hype, superlatives and pressure ("revolutionary", "#1", "guaranteed", "best in Ghana").

## 6. Approved descriptions

**Short (about 160 characters):**
KwikLearn is building a digital learning platform for Ghanaian education, starting with BECE and WASSCE practice, selected study notes and progress tracking.

**Extended:**
KwikLearn is a growing digital education platform for Ghanaian learners, developed by KwikLearn Technologies Ltd, a registered Ghanaian software development company. Our mission is to make quality education more accessible, affordable, engaging and effective through digital technology. Our journey begins with BECE and WASSCE preparation: the current Android app offers practice quizzes, exam mode, study notes for selected topics, and results and progress tracking. Our long-term vision is to build a comprehensive online learning institution that supports students, teachers, parents and educational institutions across Ghana, Africa and eventually beyond.

**Company (legal) description:**
KwikLearn Technologies Ltd is a registered Ghanaian software development company that builds mobile applications and digital solutions for individuals, businesses and organisations, as well as its own software products, including the KwikLearn digital education platform.

## 7. Website, SEO and social positioning

- Homepage title: `KwikLearn Ghana | Digital Learning, BECE & WASSCE Preparation`.
- Homepage H1: "Building a Better Future Through Digital Learning."
- Meta, Open Graph, Twitter and JSON-LD descriptions must say the same thing as the short description above. Change them together.
- Natural keywords: KwikLearn Ghana, digital education in Ghana, online learning in Ghana, BECE preparation, WASSCE preparation, educational technology, digital learning platform, study resources, school partnerships, African digital education. No keyword stuffing.
- Keep canonical URLs, analytics, Google Play links and the launch-state configuration in `js/home.js` unchanged unless the task says otherwise.
- Social bios use the short description (shortened to the platform limit), the slogan, and the Google Play link. They must not say "Mathematics app" or "exam app".

## 8. Institutional partnerships

Invite conversations with schools, teachers, preparation centres and educational NGOs about **potential partnerships and pilot opportunities**. Contact: info@kwiklearn.app (WhatsApp +233 24 427 2204 is also published).

Do not claim, unless verified in writing by the owner: existing school partnerships, school subscriptions, school or teacher dashboards, school-management tools, institutional pricing, or named pilot schools.

## 9. Current features vs future plans

Use three clearly separate buckets and never blur them.

| Bucket | Rule | Wording |
|---|---|---|
| **Available now** | Only what the live Android app does today and a person has verified. Currently: practice quizzes, exam mode, study notes for selected topics, results and progress tracking, Mistake Book. Subject availability changes, so send readers to "the Subjects section in the app" instead of listing subjects. | "Available now", "The app offers" |
| **Under development** | Only if the owner has confirmed it. Otherwise do not list it. | "We are working on" |
| **Future objectives** | Strategy and vision, never a promise or date. | "We aim to", "Our long-term vision", "We plan to explore" |

## 10. Accuracy rules and prohibited claims

Never state or imply, without verified evidence:
- That all subjects, video lessons, AI tutors, school dashboards, rewards, competitions or institutional subscriptions exist.
- Student, user or download numbers; exam results; rankings; "market leader"; awards.
- Guaranteed exam success, grades or admission outcomes.
- That KwikLearn is an accredited or registered school, university or exam body, or is endorsed by WAEC, GES, NaCCA or the Ministry of Education.
- Partnerships with named schools, organisations or government bodies.
- Pre-launch language: beta testing, waiting lists, "coming soon" for the app itself. The app is live on Google Play.
- Real classrooms, events or users in images that are illustrations. Keep "Illustrative image" captions and the screenshot disclaimers ("sample data", "Mathematics only") until screenshots are replaced with real current ones.

If a fact cannot be verified, leave it out or point readers to the app or to info@kwiklearn.app.

## 11. Guidance for AI-generated content

**Articles:** open with the reader's need, not the brand. When describing KwikLearn use the approved descriptions. Mention BECE and WASSCE as the starting point. Include only features from section 9 "Available now". End with a link to https://kwiklearn.app and the Google Play listing. No invented statistics, testimonials or quotes.

**Promotional videos:** show real app screens only (or clearly labelled illustrations). Script the mission first, features second. Say "available on Google Play for Android". No fake testimonials, no guaranteed outcomes, no on-screen numbers unless verified.

**Automated social posts:** one idea per post, tone as section 5, slogan allowed, link to kwiklearn.app. Rotate topics: study tips, platform mission, app features, school partnership invitations, Ghanaian education context. No more than a minority of posts may be pure exam-prep promotion. Never post claims from section 10.

**Product descriptions and store listings:** describe the platform first, then the current app features. State that subject availability is shown in the app. Keep Play Store limits (short 80 characters, full 4,000).

## 12. Ghanaian and African context

Write for Ghana first. Use Ghanaian spelling conventions (British English: "practise" as a verb, "organisation"), refer to JHS, SHS, BECE, WASSCE and WAEC accurately, and be sensitive to data costs, device limits and the range of school types. Show that KwikLearn is Ghanaian-built and growing toward an African ambition without claiming a presence outside Ghana. Use inclusive imagery (students of different abilities, genders and regions).

## 13. When to update this file

Update it whenever a feature launches, a verified partnership exists, the contact policy changes, or a claim in section 10 becomes true. Change the "Available now" list only after confirming the feature in the live app.
