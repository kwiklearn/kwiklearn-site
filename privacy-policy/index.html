Privacy Policy

How the KwikLearn app collects, uses, and protects your data.

Effective date: November 7, 2026
Home / Privacy Policy

KwikLearn is an educational learning app that helps students prepare for BECE and WASSCE with study notes, practice quizzes, exam mode, results, and progress tracking. The current Android beta includes Mathematics beta content, with more subjects coming gradually. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices. If you have questions, contact us at support@kwiklearn.app.

This policy is issued by KwikLearn Technologies Ltd (company registration number CS148290726), Block 80, Wachild Estate, Amasaman, Ga West Municipal, Greater Accra Region, Ghana, which decides how and why your personal data is processed (the “data controller”). Contact: support@kwiklearn.app.

KwikLearn Technologies Ltd is registered with the Data Protection Commission of Ghana under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843): certificate number PROV2608208, valid from 13 August 2026 to 12 August 2028. Registration does not mean the Commission has approved any particular way in which we process personal data.

1

What Data We Collect

  • Account details — only if you choose to sign in or create an account: your email address and password. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we cannot see it.
  • Student profile — when you create an account: your first and last name, an optional preferred name, your exam (BECE or WASSCE), level, region and school name.
  • Learning progress — which subjects and topics you've viewed or completed.
  • Quiz attempts and results — the questions you've answered, your answers, and your scores. When you are signed in, these are saved to your account; otherwise they stay on your device. Daily Challenge progress is stored on your device.
  • Payment-related records (earlier test builds only) — KwikLearn payments are switched off in the current Free version. Earlier test builds offered MTN Mobile Money payment, and may have created records containing the Mobile Money phone number, amount, a payment reference and status, together with related access (entitlement) records linked to the account.
  • Basic app usage related to learning progress — for example, which topic or quiz screen you're using, so the app can show your progress accurately.

You can use KwikLearn to browse subjects and take quizzes without creating an account. In that case, your progress is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

2

What We Do Not Collect

  • Precise GPS or live device location — KwikLearn does not request GPS coordinates or track your device’s real-time location. We do collect the region and school name you provide during registration, as described above.
  • Contacts
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Card or bank-account details — KwikLearn does not collect these
  • Advertising identifiers for ads — KwikLearn does not show ads
3

How We Use Your Data

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure
  • To save your learning progress so you can pick up where you left off, including across devices when you're signed in
  • To improve the learning experience — for example, understanding which topics need more or better questions
  • To provide support when you contact us
4

Data Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell your data to advertisers or any other third party. We do not run advertising in the app.

We use service providers to operate KwikLearn: Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Google Cloud) for sign-in and data storage; and Hostinger for the servers that run KwikLearn’s student-account service. They process personal data for us under their service terms. Our Cloud Firestore database is located in Johannesburg, South Africa. Because these providers operate internationally, your data may be processed outside Ghana.

In earlier test builds that offered payments, MTN Mobile Money processed those payments.

We may disclose personal data where the law requires it.

5

Children and Learners

KwikLearn is made for learners preparing for the BECE and WASSCE, and many users are under 18. The Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) treats personal data about a child under parental control as special personal data (section 37). We therefore collect only what we need to provide the service (see “What Data We Collect”), we do not show advertising, we do not use learners’ data for marketing, and we do not sell it.

KwikLearn does not currently verify a learner’s age, and it does not collect or verify a parent’s or guardian’s consent when an account is created. We encourage learners under 18 to use the app with the knowledge of a parent, guardian or teacher.

A parent or guardian can write to support@kwiklearn.app to ask about, correct or delete a child’s data. We will ask for confirmation at the email address registered to the account before we act.

6

Data Security and Incidents

Data sent to and from KwikLearn is transmitted over secure connections (HTTPS). Access to stored data is restricted by account sign-in, database security rules and server-side checks.

If we have reasonable grounds to believe that personal data has been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, we will notify the Data Protection Commission and the people affected as soon as reasonably practicable, as Act 843 requires, and take steps to restore the security of our systems.

7

Data Retention

We keep your account and learning-progress data while your account is active, or as needed to provide the service to you. When you delete your account, we delete that data. Copies may remain in our backups and point-in-time recovery history for up to 7 days after deletion.

Certain financial or transaction records, where applicable, may be retained for as long as required by Ghanaian tax, accounting or other applicable laws. We have not set a specific retention period for them in this policy.

8

Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Account & Settings → Privacy & Account → Delete account & data. You will be asked for your password again. Deleting removes your sign-in account, your student profile, your quiz attempts and learning progress, and any access (entitlement) record held for your account.

If you cannot access the app, email support@kwiklearn.app from the email address registered to your account with a request to delete your account and associated learning progress. We act only on requests from the registered address; if a request comes from a different address, we will ask you to confirm at the registered address first. We aim to acknowledge email requests within 2 business days and to complete them within 7 calendar days after we have successfully verified your identity by email.

Certain financial or transaction records, where applicable, may be retained for as long as required by Ghanaian tax, accounting, or other applicable laws. In the app, deleting your account removes your phone number from those records and replaces your account identifier with a random one. Payment references and amounts remain and may still be matched to Mobile Money provider records.

See our Account Deletion page for full details on what is deleted, what may remain, and expected response times.

9

Required and Optional Information

To create an account you must give your email address and a password, your first and last name, your exam, level, region and school name. Your preferred name is optional. If you do not provide the required information we cannot create an account or save your progress to it. You can still browse subjects and take quizzes without an account; in that case progress stays on your device.

10

Your Rights

Under Act 843 you may, among other things:

  • ask us to confirm whether we hold personal data about you, and to describe it and who has had access to it (sections 32 and 35). We aim to respond promptly and within 40 days, as section 35 requires;
  • ask us to correct or delete personal data that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained, or that we no longer have authority to keep (section 33);
  • object to the processing of your personal data (section 20); and
  • ask us to stop processing that causes or is likely to cause you unwarranted damage or distress (section 39).

To make a request, email support@kwiklearn.app from the address registered to your account. We may ask you to prove your identity first. You can also delete your account yourself in the app.

11

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your personal data, please write to support@kwiklearn.app so that we can try to put it right. You may also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana (dpc.gov.gh).

12

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as KwikLearn evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the "Effective date" above.

Have Questions?

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us at support@kwiklearn.app. For general enquiries, reach us at info@kwiklearn.app.
