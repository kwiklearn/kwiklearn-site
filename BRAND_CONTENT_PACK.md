# KwikLearn Brand Content Pack

Ready-to-paste copy for external platforms. Source of truth: `BRAND_GUIDELINES.md` (identity in Part A, availability register in Part B). Regenerated 2026-11-24 for the permanent brand identity.

How to use it:
- **Corporate and brand profiles** (LinkedIn, Facebook About, YouTube channel, Explee, company profile, press, media kit) present the full educational ecosystem as strategic scope and say what is available today.
- **Product-bound copy** (Google Play, feature posts, ads, app videos) names only features that are Publicly available in the guidelines' register. Anything else is labelled as not currently available.
- When a feature ships, update guidelines Part B, then refresh only the "available today" lines here. Do not rewrite the corporate text.
- Nothing here has been applied to any external platform. Applying it needs the owner's logins. Tracking table at the end.

Links: website https://kwiklearn.app, Google Play https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kwiklearn.app, partnerships info@kwiklearn.app, support support@kwiklearn.app.

Standard statement: **Feature and content availability varies by platform and release.**
