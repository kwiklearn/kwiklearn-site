# KwikLearn website: instructions for AI assistants

Before writing or changing any public content (copy, metadata, structured data, FAQs, articles, social posts, press, outreach), read `BRAND_GUIDELINES.md` in this repository. It is the single authoritative brand reference. Do not create competing brand documents and do not contradict it from older drafts. Ready-made platform copy is in `BRAND_CONTENT_PACK.md`.

Key rules (full detail in the guidelines):
- KwikLearn is a comprehensive digital education platform (KwikLearn Technologies Ltd). Its ecosystem covers structured learning, video lessons, AI-powered tutoring, examination preparation, study materials, personalised assessments, daily challenges, competitions, leaderboards, rewards, gifts, recognition, teacher participation, parent engagement and institutional partnerships.
- Examination preparation is one component. Mathematics, BECE/WASSCE or the Android app must never define the whole brand. Avoid temporary framing ("building a platform", "starting with Mathematics", "coming soon" as a brand description).
- Two layers: Part A (identity) is stable; Part B (availability register) changes per release. When a feature ships, update Part B and the site's "Available now" panel only.
- Product-bound content (Google Play, ads, feature calls to action) mentions only Publicly available features. Any mention of an unavailable feature says it is not currently available. Standard statement: "Feature and content availability varies by platform and release."
- Never claim unverified features, accreditation, partnerships, school dashboards or subscriptions, user numbers, rankings or guaranteed results.
- Keep the Google Play URL, the launch-state config in `js/home.js`, analytics and canonical URLs unchanged unless asked.
- Emails: info@kwiklearn.app (general and partnerships), support@kwiklearn.app (support, privacy, deletion). See `docs/company-contact-policy.md` in the kwiklearn-app repository.
